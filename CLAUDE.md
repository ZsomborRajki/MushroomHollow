# Mushroom Hollow

Flyff-inspired iOS action RPG on the forest floor under one giant tree. Apple-only (iOS 27+, iPhone + iPad, landscape).
Offline first; online (authoritative Swift server) later.

## Architecture rule (do not break)

- `Packages/GameCore` is the authoritative simulation: pure Swift, **no RealityKit/SwiftUI/UIKit/simd imports** (Foundation only), so it can run on a Linux server later. Deterministic: fixed 20 Hz tick, `SeededRandom` only, iterate entities in sorted-ID order.
- The app never mutates game state directly. It sends `PlayerCommand`s through a `WorldHost` and renders `WorldSnapshot`s. `LocalWorldHost` runs the sim in-process; a future `RemoteWorldHost` will talk to the server.
- RealityKit is presentation only (models, interpolation, effects). `WorldMap` is shared, so the rendered world and the collision world always match.
- Input devices (GameController, keyboard, touch) are merged into `InputFrame` (analog + discrete `InputAction`s) in `InputHub`; gameplay never reads devices.
- The sim reports what happened as `WorldEvent`s (damage, deaths, level ups...); the client turns them into effects, damage numbers, and haptics. Private player data (XP, MP, cooldowns) travels as `WorldSnapshot.viewer`.
- Tuning and content live in GameCore: `Stats.swift` (mob stats, XP curve: quick to 15, then each level ×(1 + 0.12·(level−15)) longer; from level 10 fainting costs 4% of the level's XP, never a level), `Attributes.swift` (Flyff-style STR/STA/DEX/INT: 3 points per level, spent with `spendStatPoints` and stored as points spent, so unspent = earned − spent; STR attack, STA HP/defense, DEX attack speed/crit, INT MP/skill power), `Skills.swift`, `Items.swift` (incl. `WeaponType`: sword/axe for anyone, maul/bow/wand/staff per class; each sets attack speed, reach, two-handedness; shields add block chance; `Gear` = item + upgrade level), `Sets.swift` (4-piece armor sets with 2/3/4-piece bonuses: Dewleaf at level 5, one class set at 15, Thistledown at 20), `Upgrades.swift` (+1…+10 at the blacksmith: Amber Shards from mobs + caps, rising failure risk, Ward Charms protect), `Loot.swift`, `Giants.swift`, `Quests.swift`, `NPCs.swift`, `WorldMap.swift` (zones, spawns, NPC placement, the fountain, signposts). Client-only looks (icons, colors, messages) are in `Game/SkillPresentation.swift` and `Game/ItemPresentation.swift`.
- The world (radius 300, we're tiny: daisies tower over you) is laid out like Flyff's Flaris: Capstone Town (a fountain plaza between the two southern roots, every NPC facing it) and, between the roots, Flaris-style single-species fields walked in level order (levels 1–15, three per root gap via `fields` in `WorldMap`: snail 1, ladybug 2, aphid 3, slug 4, pill bug 5, earthworm 6, beetle 8, acornling 9, cricket 11, spore beast 12, bog frog 13; Great Bough boss), each with a `Signpost` ("Slugs · Lv 4–5"). Dewdrop Lake lies southwest of the town, and an outer ring of six hunting grounds on a road at radius 190 (levels 14–30, two species per ground, each in its own half via `pair`) sits inside a bowl whose rim rises at the edge. Every field also has one Giant (`Giants.swift`: ×1.8 size, +2 levels, ×6 HP/XP, never aggressive, 5 min respawn, guaranteed caps and gear; auto-targeting skips them). Mobs spawn as far from others as a few tries find, each with its own home spot and a short wander radius, so fights are usually 1v1. Mobs have lots of HP (long fights) and modest attack. Only about one in five per spawn area is aggressive (`MobBrain.aggressive`, a per-area quota refilled on respawn; `EntitySnapshot.isAggressive`); the rest only fight back, and snails, ladybugs, and aphids never aggro.
- Draw calls: models are built from primitive parts, then `PartMerger` merges unnamed, childless, untextured sibling parts per joint (in ink, all toon colors go through the shared `PartAtlas` texture, so a joint is one mesh + one hull). Name a part (or give it a texture) to keep it separate if it moves, swaps material, or is refit on its own (wings, `bowString`, `swingTrail`, the face). Mob models are built once per kind and cloned; player rigs call `Record.restore()` before `dress` and merge again after. The town and forge are flattened into per-40 m cells. The renderer places the camera before posing actors (culling uses this frame's view) and skips off-screen actors.
- Terrain (`Terrain.swift`): a pure-math heightfield (rolling ground + `Hill`s + `FlatArea`s + lakes carved in). Sim `position.y` is altitude **above the ground**; the client adds `groundHeight`/`standingHeight`. Deep water blocks walkers (`waterColliders`) but gliders hover over it at `waterHoverAltitude`. Scenery that you bump into (`Plant`s, `Boulder`s, `Twig`s, all in `Scenery.swift`) is scattered deterministically by `SceneryLayout` and collides via a `ColliderGrid`; `Trail`s are kept clear.
- Mob abilities are traits in `MobAI.swift` (`hidesWhenHurt`, `charges`, `trail`, `cloud`, `splitsInto`), so a new mob just picks them; hazards (`slime`, `web`, `embers`, clouds) are tuned per `HazardKind`. Every mob drops its own material; each zone's mobs drop the next tier of common gear.
- NPC interactions (shops, quests, upgrades at Shiitake, Porcini's material trade) are sim commands validated by distance, so they'll work unchanged online. Shops as in Flarine: Chanterelle's general store (potion tiers, Blinkwings that return you to town when not in combat, the Dandelion Seed), Oyster's weapon shop and Enoki's armor shop (a gear tier every few levels up to ~21; higher tiers are drop-only). Every shop has a Buyback tab (`Buyback.swift`): your last 12 sales, shared by all shopkeepers, bought back whole at the price they fetched; not saved.
- Economy (Flyff-style, money comes from quests): a mob drops a small caps pile (a few caps per level) only 40% of the time, materials sell cheaply, and mobs 8+ levels below you drop only their material. Story quests pay the most; Maitake's request board (`QuestID.huntingRequests`, one per species, repeatable, 12 kills, hidden once you're 8+ levels past the field) is the steady income.
- Quests: one linear main story (`QuestID.mainStory`, each quest's prerequisite is the one before), one species per quest in level order around the tree, handed out in turn by the townsfolk; the owl and Truffle's pet quest are side quests, and the hunting requests are repeatable (`isRepeatable`, never added to `completedQuests`). Saves store `QuestID` raw values: never rename or remove a case. Porcini the naturalist (`Bounties.swift`) buys every mob's own material (`ItemID.bounty`: sell price in caps plus a third of the mob's XP, scaled by level difference).
- Saves: the sim exposes `PlayerProfile` (Codable); the app stores it with SwiftData in `Persistence/SaveStore.swift` (autosave every 20 s, on key events, and when backgrounded). New profile fields must be optional (or decode with defaults) so older saves still load.
- Time of day comes from the sim tick (`WorldSnapshot.timeOfDay`), so online players will share one sky. `Rendering/Atmosphere.swift` turns it into lighting, sky shader parameters, and `ColorGradeEffect` (RealityKit post-process + `Shaders/PostProcess.metal`).
- Art style (`Rendering/InkStyle.swift`, `ArtStyle.current`, chosen at launch from UserDefaults `artStyle`, default `ink`; client-only): the **ink** style is hand-drawn. `Materials.matte`/`glossy`/`translucent`, the scenery atlas, ground, grass, water, and face route to unlit toon `CustomMaterial`s (two-tone fill, cool shadows, boiling screen-space hatching, contour ink, cartoon shine). They're lit by one shared 4×1 `ToonLighting` texture that `Atmosphere` rewrites as the day turns, so there are no shadow maps. Actors get inverted-hull outlines (`addInkHulls`) and hatched blob shadows (`InkPainter`), and the post-process adds boiling depth/color-edge lines, chalk lines at night, and paper grain. Everything boils at `InkStyle.boilRate` (8 fps, matching `boilFrame` in `World.metal`; frames wrap at 1024 and shader hashes are integer, never `sin`, so noise stays stable in long sessions). The post-process depth lines take the second difference of raw depth on whole, symmetric texels (flat ground at any angle must score exactly zero; uneven taps drew boiling smoke-like blotches on device). Ink scenery's hand-drawn wobble is baked into the meshes (`InkWobble`); ink scenes have no lights (all materials are unlit); ambient spores are drawn motes (`InkPainter.mote`), not additive glows. **classic** is the original lit PBR look. The post-process draws a full-screen triangle (not compute: drawables can be sRGB/XR formats compute can't write, and the Simulator lacks non-uniform threadgroups). The Simulator's post-process depth buffer is empty, so depth lines and fog only show on device.
- Pets (`GameCore/Pets.swift`, Flyff-style pickup pets): a passive companion that never fights. Pip the pup is the reward for Truffle's quest (level 3, after Shell Shock); the pet item goes in the pet slot (`PlayerData.pet`), then summon/dismiss. Pets are not entities: each owner's pet lives in `PetData.body` and reaches clients as `WorldSnapshot.pets` (one per owner, ID = owner), so combat, AI, and targeting never see them. It trots behind its owner and fetches the owner's own drops within 7 m (via `grantDrop`, skipping what won't fit in the bag), hides while the owner flies or has fainted, and pops back beside them past 25 m. Fullness drains only while it's out (30 min full, 3 min per Kibble); at a quarter it's hungry (55% speed), at zero it goes home until fed (feeding brings it back). Kibble (stacks to 200) comes from Truffle, who bakes mob materials at `ItemID.kibbleValue` each (1 + 2/5 of the sell price). Client: `Rendering/PetModels.swift` (`PetRig`, Truffle), `WorldRenderer.renderPets`.
- World boss logic is in `GameCore/WorldBoss.swift` (spawn once per night via `nightIndex`, scripted `BossBrain` moves, `TelegraphSnapshot`s for the client). Everyone in `BossBrain.damagers` gets XP/loot/quest credit.
- Game Center (`Services/GameCenter.swift`): achievement IDs `org.mushroomhollow.achievement.{first_flight, chose_a_path, level_10, level_30, owl_slayer}` must exist in App Store Connect. Entitlement in `Config/MushroomHollow.entitlements`.
- Audio is synthesized at launch (`Audio/SoundSynth.swift` → WAV `Data` → `AudioFileResource(from:)`); `SoundBank` plays combat sounds spatially from entities.

## Layout

- `MushroomHollow/` app target (Xcode synchronized folder: new files are picked up automatically)
  - `App/` SwiftUI entry and `GameView` (RealityView + overlays)
  - `Game/` `GameSession` (per-frame glue, panels, event → feedback), `OrbitCamera`
  - `Rendering/` `WorldRenderer`, `WorldBuilder` (static world), `SceneryBatch` (merges scenery into one palette-atlas material per chunk, culled by distance), `Flora` (giant flowers, ferns, rocks, twigs), `TerrainBuilder` (ground tiles, skirt, water, grass chunks), `GroundPainter` (the ground texture, also the map background), `ActorModels` (mob primitives; the newer critters are in `CritterModels`, all with big anime eyes via `addCuteEyes`), `PlayerRig` (the player "Sprout" in Flyff proportions, about four heads tall; townsfolk reuse it with a `Look` and a mushroom cap from `NPCLooks`; body armor reshapes the outfit via `BodyStyle`: tunic/mail/robe/coat; joint hierarchy, procedural walk/cast/cheer animation, a per-weapon-type attack: sword/axe/maul/wand swing combos with trails, bow draw/release, staff thrust+twirl, shield block; gear on joints), `WeaponModels` (weapon and shield meshes), `TownBuilder` (fountain, lamps, shop stalls with signboards, request board, field signposts), `Nameplate` (floating "Lv 3 Aphid" names over mobs colored by level gap, and NPC names), `FacePainter` (anime face painted with CoreGraphics into a head texture, one material per expression), `InkStyle` (art style switch, toon lighting, ink materials, hull outlines), `InkPainter` (hand-drawn ink textures), `PetModels` (pet rigs with trot/idle/hungry/fetch animation, the pet keeper), `Placeholders` (palette, meshes incl. lathe/torus/uvSphere, materials)
  - `Shaders/` Metal: `CustomMaterial` shaders (grass sway/color, day/night sky, the ink style's toon/hull/ground shaders) and the post-process pass (grade, fog, ink lines)
  - `Audio/` sound synthesis and playback
  - `Input/` (`InputHub`: gameplay vs menu button mapping), `HUD/` (HUD, `ActionBar`, `Panels` for bag / NPC dialogs, `MapPanel` + `MapPainter` for the world map), `Persistence/`
- `Packages/GameCore/` simulation + Swift Testing tests
- `Config/Info.plist` holds only keys that have no build setting (GameController keys); everything else is `INFOPLIST_KEY_*`.

Conventions: yaw 0 faces +Z and yaw `a` faces (sin a, cos a); models face +Z. The app target uses default MainActor isolation and `MemberImportVisibility`, so import every module you use.

## Commands

- Sim tests: `cd Packages/GameCore && swift test`
- App build: `xcodebuild -project MushroomHollow.xcodeproj -scheme MushroomHollow -destination 'generic/platform=iOS Simulator' build` (add `-configuration Debug` when testing debug launch arguments: `xcodebuild` builds Release by default, which compiles them out)
- Simulator controls (hardware keyboard): WASD move, Q/E or arrows camera, Space/F attack, Tab next target, Esc clear, 1/2/3 skills.
- Keyboard extras: 1–5 skills, 6/7 potions, I bag, M map, G fly/land, R/C climb/descend, P summon/dismiss pet, T character (stat points); in menus arrows/WASD navigate, Space/Enter confirm, Esc back, X sell a whole stack, Q/E switch shop tab (Buy/Sell/Buyback; at the forge: toggle Ward Charm; on the map: Space cycles zoom, Q/E zoom, WASD pan). Touch: pinch/drag the map, double-tap to re-center; the top-right row has mute, character (badge = unspent points), and map buttons.
- Controller: left stick move, right stick camera, A attack/talk/respawn, X/Y/B skills, RT+X/RT+Y class skills, LB/RB cycle target, LT clear target, d-pad up/down zoom, d-pad left/right potions, Menu bag, View/Options map, L3 fly/land (RT/LT climb/descend while flying). In panels: d-pad/stick navigate, A confirm, B back, X sell a whole stack, LB/RB shop tabs (in the bag: switch to the character panel). Character panel: up/down pick an attribute, right/A add a point, left removes one, Confirm spends them.
- Debug launch arguments (DEBUG builds only; all but `-resetSave` use a throwaway in-memory save): `-autofight`, `-demo` (geared level 10 character with a Dandelion Seed, upgraded gear, Dewleaf pieces, and amber), `-level N` (stat points left unspent; `-demo`/`-class`/`-weapon` characters come with theirs spent), `-class guardian|thornshot|sporecaster|dewkeeper` (level 18, wielding the class weapon), `-weapon sword|axe|maul|bow|wand|staff` (plus a buckler for one-handers), `-attackloop` (attack thin air on repeat, blocking every 5th; pair with `-portrait <degrees>`), `-time 0...1` (0 midnight, 0.5 noon), `-spawn village|lake|glade|maze|barkfall|fen|meadow|creek|thicket|rise|briars|grove`, `-panel bag|stats|morel|shop|sell|buyback|weapons|armor|board|smith|truffle|porcini|map`, `-fly`, `-pet [hungry]` (Pip out and following, with Kibble; `hungry` starts it 5 s from slowing down), `-owl` (summon the world boss and stand in its arena), `-portrait [degrees]` (camera close up in front of the player, optionally orbited; view only), `-style ink|classic` (art style, view only), `-resetSave`. Example: `xcrun simctl launch <udid> org.mushroomhollow.MushroomHollow -demo -panel shop`.
